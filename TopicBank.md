Project Title: BudgetWise: A Machine Learning-Based Student Budget Tracking and Expense Prediction System

Idea: A web-based budget tracking system that uses machine learning to analyze students' spending habits and predict their possible expenses for the upcoming week or month.

Problem it addresses: College students who receive daily or weekly allowances often struggle to monitor their expenses and avoid overspending. Traditional budget trackers record expenses but may not help students anticipate their future spending.

Closest existing work:
Student Budget Planner (2026) – Uses machine learning and Linear Regression to predict student expenses.
YNAB – An existing budgeting application that provides expense tracking, budget planning, and spending reports.

Data needed: Students weekly or monthly allowances, daily expense amounts, expense categories (food, transportation, school supplies, and personal needs), transaction dates, and previous spending records.

Compute needed:Laptop, Python, Jupyter Notebook and/or Google Colab, machine learning libraries, PHP, and MySQL.

Biggest risk: Obtaining enough accurate and consistent student spending records to train and evaluate the machine learning model, since students have different allowances and spending habits.

Feasibility: 4/5
